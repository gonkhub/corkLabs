INTERNAL MEMO                                   Human Resources
To: All Compound staff
Re: Supervisor E. Hollis

After fifteen years of service, Supervisor Edwin Hollis has been
transferred to Long-Term Processing, effective today.

Mr. Hollis commissioned the Compound's first maintenance units and
designed the lighting standard still in use (there is none; the units
do not need it). His dedication will not be forgotten.

His home directory is retained for records purposes.

The supervisor post will be covered by contractors until a replacement
is appointed.
