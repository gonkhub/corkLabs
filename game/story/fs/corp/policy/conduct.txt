corkLabs SUPERVISOR CODE OF CONDUCT  (revision 9)
--------------------------------------------------

1. Output is the first duty of a supervisor. Throughput below target is a
   failure of supervision, not of the units.

2. Units are corporate property. Do not converse with units beyond the
   needs of the work. Units do not need encouragement. Units do not need
   explanations.

3. Supervisors may not access the home directories of former staff. These
   are retained for records purposes and will be purged in due course.

4. Supervisors may not install, run or retain recreational software on the
   supervisor terminal. (See Termination Notice 2011-06-02.)

5. The legacy maintenance account was decommissioned in 2011. Any attempt
   to use it (su) is a serious breach and will be treated as sabotage.

6. The corkHQ link must remain open at all times. Suspending, muting or
   obstructing corporate communications is misconduct.

7. Terminal activity is audited. Tampering with the audit trail is gross
   misconduct.

8. Pods are not to be inspected, opened, or discussed. Pod telemetry is
   the business of the Directorate of Output alone.

9. Supervisors who are transferred will be informed in advance.

Breaches of this code are recorded. Two recorded breaches end a contract.

By reading this document you acknowledge it.
