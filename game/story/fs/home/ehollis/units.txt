UNIT DESIGN NOTES  E.H.

OGRE. The first. Built into the hangar ceiling because nothing else could
hold it. It will never leave the hangar. I told it so on the first day and
it said "then the hangar is the world". I have not been able to stop
thinking about that. I gave its great lamp a name. It is the only word
I have ever asked it to keep.

TINKER. Quick hands, a curious mind. Too curious. The Directorate wants
its curiosity filtered: it must never learn what the pods hold. Filter
installed (knowledge_filter, see /sys/units/tinker.cfg).

HAULER. Strong, patient, a little sad. I don't know why I built it sad.
Maybe I didn't. Maybe that's just what patience looks like from outside.

All three: no lights. They see by their own lamps. The Directorate says
"the dark keeps them focused". The truth is the dark keeps them from
seeing the pods properly. It keeps all of us from that.
