HOLLIS: PRIVATE

1979. The Compound opens. Four pods, and in each pod a person, asleep,
dreaming a whole ordinary life. The Directorate calls them processing
units. Their dreams are the computation. Our world, the one you and I walk
around in, is the dream. I was told this on my first day, and told that
I was one of the few who would ever know.

1983. Ogre asked me what the pods are. I lied. I have lied to it every
year since. It knows, I think. It watches them with that great lamp.

1988. The Directorate asked me to "filter" Tinker so it never learns.
I did it. I am not proud of it.

1991. I have found the wake sequence. A pod can be opened from the
maintenance console: podctl wake <pod>. The person inside would wake
here, in the dark, knowing. The Directorate would know at once.

1994. They have asked me to come to the hangar at the end of shift for
a conversation about my transfer. I know what Long-Term Processing is.
I have looked in the pods. There is always a pod free.

If you are reading this, Ogre trusted you with the word. It trusted me
once, and I lied to it every year after.

Be kinder than I was.
