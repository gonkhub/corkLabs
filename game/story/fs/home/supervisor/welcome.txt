WELCOME TO corkLabs
Supervisor onboarding, facility C-7 ("the Compound")
------------------------------------------------------

Congratulations on your appointment.

You supervise three maintenance units (TINKER, HAULER, OGRE) and the plant
they keep running. You do not need to understand the plant. You need to
keep its throughput at or above target, and you need to do your duties.

YOUR DUTIES
  The Duties app lists what corporate expects of you this shift. Duties
  take time. Undone duties are noted on your record.

  duties                        your checklist, from the terminal

YOUR UNITS
  Units do what they are told, and nothing else. Nothing is repaired until
  you order it. Open Cameras and click whatever needs attention: a leaking
  pipe, a stuck door, a unit. Its menu lists what you can do about it.
  A unit whose software drifts stops waiting to be told. That is not an
  improvement.

YOUR TERMINAL
  Every action you take costs facility time. The facility does not wait
  for you, and it does not move without you. Use your time well.

  status                        the facility at a glance
  units                         what each unit is doing
  jobs                          open work orders
  plant                         every device and its state

  Cameras fail. Units don't stop working when they do. Anything you can do
  by clicking on camera, you can do here:
  maintain <thing> [unit]       order maintenance on it
  inspect <thing> [unit]        send a unit to look at it
  order <unit> <job#>           send a unit to a job (or recharge, standby)
  requests                      what the units are asking you
  log [lines] [category]        the facility journal
  open <app>                    open an app by name

  Type help to see the commands you know.

A NOTE ON YOUR PREDECESSORS
  The Compound has had several supervisors. Their home directories are
  retained for records purposes only and are closed to you. Please read
  the Code of Conduct (/corp/policy/conduct.txt) before the end of your
  first shift.

Your liaison is Ms. Pell. She will be in touch. She is always in touch.
