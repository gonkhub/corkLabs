# Liaison Pell (the corkHQ panel's Reply button). Corporate's voice with a
# face: polite, watchful, never quite answering. What you say to her moves
# your standing and suspicion. Format: see game/story/dialogue.gd.

== start
-> {!met:pell} first
pell: Supervisor.
-> menu

== first
~ learn met:pell
pell: Supervisor. You found the reply button. Most don't, for a while.
pell: I'm here to help you succeed. Success here is simple: numbers up, questions down.
-> menu

== menu
* {directive:explain} About my activity: I can explain. -> explain
* {ogre_down & !form:c9} Unit OGRE is down. I need a replacement core. -> ogre_core
* How am I doing? -> perf
* {!seen:pell.okafor} What happened to the last supervisor? -> okafor
* {transfer:okafor & !seen:pell.transfer} What is Long-Term Processing? -> transfer
* {!seen:pell.door} Where's the door? -> door
* {secret:former_staff & !seen:pell.confess} I read the former staff files. -> confess
* {shift>=2 & !purged:dokafor & !seen:pell.purge} Why purge Okafor's files? -> purge
* {secret:pod3 & !seen:pell.pod3} I know who's in pod 3. -> pod3
* {secret:hq_silence & !seen:pell.silence} Did you notice the link went quiet? -> silence
* Nothing. (Close the reply.) -> END

== explain
pell: Go on.
* It won't happen again. -> explain_sorry
* I was doing my job. -> explain_job
* With respect, that's my business. -> explain_no

== explain_sorry
pell: Good. I'll pass that on.
~ directive explain
~ standing 1 | apologised to the liaison
-> menu

== explain_job
pell: Your job is the numbers, Supervisor. I'll pass that on too.
~ directive explain
~ suspicion 3 | justified the activity to the liaison
-> menu

== explain_no
pell: It isn't. Noted.
~ standing -4 | refused to explain to the liaison
-> menu

== perf
-> {standing>=70} perf_good
-> {standing>=40} perf_ok
pell: Your standing is poor. I'd concentrate on the numbers. And on your duties.
-> menu

== perf_good
pell: The Directorate is pleased with you. Keep it that way.
-> menu

== perf_ok
pell: Adequate. The Directorate prefers better than adequate.
-> menu

== okafor
pell: Supervisor Okafor was transferred. Transfers are routine.
pell: They were a valued member of staff. You are a valued member of staff.
-> menu

== transfer
pell: Long-Term Processing is where supervisors go when their contribution here is complete.
pell: It's very quiet. Nobody has ever complained.
~ standing -1 | asked the liaison about transfers
-> menu

== door
pell: You won't need one.
-> menu

== confess
pell: I know. I see what you open.
* I'm sorry. It won't happen again. -> confess_sorry
* They were here before me. I wanted to know. -> confess_honest

== confess_sorry
pell: Good. Contrition is noted.
~ standing 3 | apologised to the liaison
~ learn apologised
-> menu

== confess_honest
pell: Curiosity is a quality we screen for, Supervisor. In both directions.
~ suspicion 5 | admitted reading former staff files
-> menu

== purge
pell: Because there is nothing in them you need. If there were, I would have told you.
pell: Confirm the purge in your duties. It will be noticed either way.
-> menu

== pod3
pell: ...
pell: Pods are not to be inspected, opened or discussed, Supervisor. That includes with me.
pell: Especially with me.
~ suspicion 10 | discussed pod telemetry with the liaison
-> menu

== silence
pell: The link is never quiet, Supervisor. Sometimes you just can't hear it.
~ suspicion 4 | asked the liaison about the suspended link
-> menu

== ogre_core
pell: Ogre. Yes. Its core regulator failed in the night.
pell: Essential components are supplied free of charge. On Form C-9, Replacement of an Essential Component. It's in your Forms now.
pell: Fill it in properly. The unit's serial number is in its configuration file, and the fault code comes from a diagnostic.
pell: Forms that come back wrong go to the bottom of the pile.
~ learn form:c9
-> menu
