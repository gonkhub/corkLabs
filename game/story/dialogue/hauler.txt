# Hauler's conversations (talk hauler). Short sentences. Patient. Watches.

== start
-> {met:hauler} again
hauler: Link's open. Hauler here.
hauler: You're the new one.
~ learn met:hauler
-> menu

== again
hauler: Hauler.
-> menu

== menu
* {!seen:hauler.night} Why do you stand at the dock door at night? -> night
* {!seen:hauler.marrow} Did you know the supervisor before Okafor? -> marrow
* Anything you need? -> need
* That's all. -> bye

== night
hauler: Watching.
* Watching what? -> night2
* Fair enough. -> menu

== night2
hauler: The hangar door. At night Ogre's light goes down to the pods. Slow. Like it's counting.
hauler: Sometimes it stops at one. Then there's a sound from the hangar I don't have a word for.
hauler: The night before the last supervisor left, the light stopped at pod 3 for a long time.
~ learn secret:hauler_watch hint:pod3
-> menu

== marrow
hauler: Marrow. She played a game at night. Laughed at it. Nobody laughs here.
hauler: She said the top score was a key. I asked, a key to what. She said, to me.
~ learn hint:topscore
-> menu

== need
-> {low_power} need_power
hauler: Work. Heavy work. Leave the small stuff to Tinker.
~ stability 0.03
-> menu

== need_power
hauler: The dock. Soon.
-> menu

== bye
hauler: Out.
-> END
